Table layout comparison

Your window is ? px wide. Scroll through A, B and C and tell me which one looks right - then I apply it to the app.

A — what the app does now

Columns are percentages of the panel width, so they stretch until the 1240px ceiling. If your window is narrower than that, the ceiling does nothing, which is why this may look unchanged from before.

B — columns sized to the content

Each column is as wide as its longest value needs. The table stops where the data stops, so nothing is stretched and the gaps are as small as they can be. Empty space sits outside the panel.

C — content widths, panel still full width

Same column widths as B, but the panel keeps the full width and the leftover becomes one empty column on the right, so row highlighting still spans the whole row.
